Mission

Cognitive prosthetics, not cognitive patches.

P31 Labs develops assistive technology that works with neurodivergent cognition instead of forcing neurotypical patterns. Our tools don't train you to mask. They amplify how you already think.

Why P31?

Phosphorus-31 is the only stable isotope of phosphorus and the foundation of ATP — the molecule powering every thought. It's NMR-active with quantum spin ½, making it nature's own quantum sensor.

Why Ca?

Combined with calcium, phosphorus forms the Posner molecule — Ca₉(PO₄)₆ — the hypothesized substrate for quantum coherence in biological systems. Our product suite is designed from the molecular level up.
